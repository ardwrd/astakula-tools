01

RGB is device-oriented

HEX and RGB describe the same sRGB color with different notation. They are the most direct formats for conventional web color values.

02

HSL is convenient, not perceptual

HSL is useful for authoring but equal numerical steps do not correspond to equal perceived lightness or chroma changes.

03

OKLCH is perceptual

OKLCH exposes lightness, chroma, and hue in a space designed to behave more uniformly for visual color adjustments.
