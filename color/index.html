01

Perceptual color space

OKLCH is used for hue relationships and tonal changes because its lightness, hue, and chroma behavior is more perceptually uniform than traditional HSL adjustments.

02

Contrast is measured

Normal text targets 4.5:1 for WCAG AA, large text targets 3:1, and 7:1 is reported as the enhanced AAA threshold for normal text.

03

Harmony is a suggestion

Complementary, analogous, split-complementary, and triadic modes describe structured hue relationships. They do not certify that a palette fits a specific brand.
